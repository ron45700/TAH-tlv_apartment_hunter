## 8. What a block looks like (question 7)

- **Blocked:** the top document's request to `www.yad2.co.il` answers `302` with `Location` on `validate.perfdrive.com`;
  that page is `200`, HTML, about 15 KB, titled "Radware Bot Manager Captcha". **No request reaches `gw.yad2.co.il` at
  all**: no map, no JSON. So in a browser, a block is told apart by the page's final host not being Yad2 and the map
  response missing, not by an empty list.
- **A result:** the map response is `200` with `"message": "OK"` and a `data.markers` list. An empty one was not
  observed; it is assumed to be `"markers": []` with `"OK"`. The clusters' `docCount` and the feed's
  `pagination.total` give a second count to compare against.
- **Not tested:** what Yad2's API answers when called directly, outside the page.

## 9. Questions for Ron

No recommendation is made on any of these.

**Gate A (`RawPost`)**
1. `group_id` is required and drives the per-group watermark and silent-group detection. Yad2 has no group. What would
   stand in its place, if anything: Yad2's region (3), a city, a neighbourhood, one saved search, or a different record?
2. `source` accepts `"thedoor"` / `"memo23"`. A third value is a Gate A change. And `listing_id = sha256(source_post_id)`
   leaves `source` out so that one Facebook post read by two providers is one record. A Yad2 `token` (8 letters and
   digits) is not a Facebook id: should `source` enter its hash?
3. `posted_at`: which of Yad2's dates is it (`rebouncedAt`, `updatedAt`, `createdAt`)? They are written without a time
   zone and read as Israel time (§3), so code would have to state the zone to make them UTC (invariant 9). And they
   exist only on the listing page (one page per listing) or in the recommendation-style responses, not on the map.
4. `raw` is stored whole: which response is a Yad2 post's `raw`? The map marker, the listing page's `data`, or both?
   And the phone response, which Yad2 sends only on a click and counts (`phone-displayed`)?

**The per-group watermark and invariant 2**
5. The map gives no date, is capped at 200 per request (100 private, 100 agency) and is not ordered by time
   (`orderId` is not sorted). A watermark of "the highest post time seen" cannot be read from it. Is a Yad2 run's
   window a watermark at all (it would need every listing's page), or a "tokens seen before" set, or something else?
   How is "a skipped window is a missed apartment" defined for Yad2?
6. Coverage: 4,460 Tel Aviv-Yafo listings against a 200-marker cap means many boxes or the list feed's pages (257 for
   region 3). How much of Yad2 is in scope: Tel Aviv-Yafo only, the 71 areas, region 3?

**Invariants 8, 13 and 14**
7. **Invariant 13 says "no cookies, with any provider."** The approach that worked is a real browser with a persistent
   profile, which keeps Yad2's own visitor cookies (no account, no login; the page also calls `gw.yad2.co.il/auth/uuid`).
   Does invariant 13, written for Facebook, cover Yad2's visitor cookies?
8. **Headless is blocked.** An unattended run on `homeserver` would need a headed browser there (on a virtual display)
   or would not run at all. No evasion was tried and none is proposed. Is a headed browser on the server acceptable to
   you, or does Yad2 stay a laptop-only or manual source?
9. Invariants 8 and 14: the description would be the text sent to the model verbatim and hashed after normalization, as
   with Facebook. But a Yad2 listing already gives price, rooms, sqm, floor, neighbourhood, broker or private and
   `inProperty` as fields. Does a Yad2 listing go through the model, only for its description, or not at all?
   (`SCHEMA.md`'s `native_*` fields, Gate B and #114's price rule were all written for Facebook.)

**Areas: Yad2's neighbourhoods against the 71**
10. Yad2's neighbourhood ids are its own (for example 1520, 1461, 991410) and unrelated to the municipal numbers. In the
    map responses Tel Aviv-Yafo showed 49 distinct Yad2 neighbourhoods; **16 names are spelled exactly like one of the
    71**, and **21 join several places in one name** ("לב תל אביב, לב העיר צפון", "התקוה, בית יעקב, נווה צה"ל"). Every
    marker has coordinates; 13 of 200 have no neighbourhood. The area could come from a hand table of Yad2 ids, the
    coordinates against the municipality's polygons (the 71's source, layer 511), the model (#167), or none. Which?

**Dedup across sources, and the rest**
11. The same apartment on Facebook and on Yad2 shares no id. The Gate D key ties two posts by a phone that is not an
    agent number, or a byte-identical photo, plus the fields. On Yad2 the phone needs a counted click, both numbers seen
    were **virtual** (`isVirtualPhoneNumber`), and the photos are re-hosted on `img.yad2.co.il`. Is cross-source dedup in
    scope, and on what?
12. Filters: Yad2's broker flag (`adType`) is the site's, not the model's; prices of 0 and above 20,000 occur; parking,
    storage and housing units are mixed into rent. Any of these for the filter rules is a change for your approval.
13. Yad2's terms of use were not examined (an open point since #60).
